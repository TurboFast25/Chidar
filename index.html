What is Qi?

In Chinese philosophy, qi (氣) is the vital life force that flows through all living things and spaces. It is the invisible energy that connects body, mind, and environment.

Feng shui — literally "wind and water" — is the practice of arranging your surroundings to harmonize with the natural flow of qi. When qi moves freely through a room, the space feels balanced, calm, and alive. When it stagnates or rushes, discomfort follows.

Qidar analyzes your room from a single photo, builds a 2D floor plan, and evaluates your furniture layout against core feng shui principles — command position, the five elements, the bagua map, yin-yang balance, and qi flow — then gives you actionable guidance to improve your space.
