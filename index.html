What is Qi?

Qi (氣) — pronounced "chee" — is the vital life force that flows through all living things, spaces, and the natural world. In Chinese philosophy, everything is animated by qi: your body, your breath, and the rooms you inhabit.

When qi flows freely through a space, it feels alive — balanced, calm, and energizing. When qi stagnates behind clutter or rushes through narrow corridors, the space feels heavy, anxious, or draining.
